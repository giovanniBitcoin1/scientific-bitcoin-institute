Pen-and-Paper Seed Worksheet

Scientific Bitcoin Institute — fully manual BIP-39 seed generation (method of Estudio Bitcoin, “Do you trust your seed? Don’t generate it yourself”). Print this page; work offline, in private, in pencil.

Procedure

  1. Flip a fair coin 128 times (12 words) or 256 times (24 words). Record heads as 1, tails as 0, left to right into the grid below. Vary your flipping technique; never redo “ugly” sequences.
  2. Each row of the grid holds 11 bits = one word. The final row is intentionally short by 4 bits (12-word) or 8 bits (24-word): those missing bits are the checksum, which must be computed by machine.
  3. For each full row, convert the 11 bits to a number 0–2047 using the place values, then look the number up in the printed word list (numbered 0–2047) included in this kit.
  4. For the final word: enter all your flips into either app in this kit offline (or an air-gapped signer with a “final word” feature — SeedSigner, SpecterDIY, Coldcard). Confirm the machine agrees with every word you derived by hand; it will supply the last word.

Checklist before funding

☐ Hand-derived words match the app’s words exactly (both editions of the app agree)
☐ Final checksum word obtained from an offline machine / air-gapped signer
☐ Phrase written on paper (or metal); no photos, no digital copies
☐ Dry-run restore on hardware wallet succeeded; small test amount sent and recovered
☐ This worksheet and all scratch paper destroyed (burn, don’t bin)
